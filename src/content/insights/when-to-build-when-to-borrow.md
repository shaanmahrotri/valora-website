A ten-person office cannot hold every capability it needs, and the ones that try tend to do many things adequately and nothing well. The harder, more useful question is which capabilities belong inside and which are better borrowed.

Keep inside what is proprietary, recurring and central to trust: relationships, judgement, the things that define how this particular family operates. Borrow what is specialised, occasional or commoditised.

### Hold judgement, rent expertise

The mistake is to confuse the two — to build a permanent function for an occasional need, or to outsource the judgement that should never leave the room.

> Hold the judgement inside the office. Rent the expertise that judgement draws on.

Capacity is not about size. It is about putting the right capability in the right place, and resisting the urge to own all of it.
